# Photo credits

The photographs in `public/images/photos/` are **placeholders**. They were chosen
to show the layout with real media in it, not because they depict Handle, its
partner hospitals, or its patients. Replace them with commissioned or licensed
photography before launch.

`hero-lobby.jpg` is the exception to everything below: it was **supplied by the
client**, not sourced here, and its rights are theirs to confirm. It is a render
of a reception lobby and does not depict a Handle location either.

Every other file is **CC0 / public domain**: no attribution is legally required
and there is no share-alike obligation. That table exists so the provenance is
recoverable, not because the licence demands it.

Each was cropped to the aspect ratio its slot declares and given one shared warm
grade (saturation 0.86, a cream soft-light pass) so a dozen unrelated sources
read as one set. The pipeline lives in the project history, not in the repo — it
was a one-off.

## Not photographed

- **Testimonials** keep the generated `MediaPlate` art. Putting a real,
  identifiable person's face beside an invented quote would present them as
  having said something they never said. That stays abstract until there are
  real patients who have consented.
- **Partner logos** are still typographic placeholders. No real hospital mark is
  reproduced anywhere on the site.
